AI Engine

Specialized agents, not a generic chatbot.

Each AI agent is purpose-built for a specific role — trained on real dealership data, your strategy profiles, and market conditions. They work alongside your team, not as a novelty.

🎯Buyer Agent
📊Inventory Agent
🔍Appraisal Agent

VinFlow's AI isn't a single model answering questions. It's a system of specialized agents that understand your inventory strategy, profit targets, market position, and buying history. Each agent operates within guardrails you configure — no black box decisions.

Buyer Agent

Scans auction run lists and scores every vehicle against your strategy profile. Identifies units that match your inventory gaps and profit targets before the lane opens.

"14 vehicles match your acquisition criteria in tomorrow's run. 3 are high-confidence buys under your max bid threshold."
Inventory Agent

Monitors aging, market shifts, and competitive pricing daily. Flags vehicles that need repricing or remarketing before carrying costs erode margin.

"6 units crossed 45 days today. 2 have dropped below cost-to-market. Repricing recommendations ready for review."
Appraisal Agent

Aggregates market data, history reports, and reconditioning estimates into a single buy/pass recommendation with confidence scoring on every data point.

"2019 RAM 1500 Laramie — Flow Score 7.2. Clean title, 1-owner. Market position: $2,400 below average. Recommend buy at $28,500."

Enterprise Security

Your data is our highest priority.

Dealerships handle sensitive financial data daily. VinFlow is built against SOC 2 controls and the FTC Safeguards Rule from day one — not as an afterthought. VinSphere is preparing for its SOC 2 Type I audit, and an independent penetration test of the platform is under way.

🔒

Encryption

Data travels over encrypted connections (TLS), and the dealer credentials we store for connected systems are encrypted with AES-256-GCM under per-dealership keys protected by AWS KMS.

🛡

FTC Safeguards

Mapped to the FTC Safeguards Rule (16 CFR 314.4). Each dealership remains responsible for its own Safeguards program.

👥

Role-Based Access

Granular RBAC with full multi-tenant isolation per dealership.

📝

Complete Audit Trail

Every action logged with user, timestamp, IP, and context for compliance.

Architecture

Modern stack. Open ecosystem.

Built on production-grade infrastructure designed for performance, scalability, and clean third-party integration.

Cloud-Native

Next.js frontend on Vercel, NestJS API on Railway, PostgreSQL with Prisma ORM. No self-hosted servers, no maintenance windows.

Provider-Agnostic Adapters

DMS integrations use an adapter pattern — plug in any DMS (Tekion, CDK, Reynolds) without changing core logic. Same pattern for data providers.

API-First Design

Every feature is API-backed. Open ecosystem for third-party integrations with per-dealership credential isolation and encrypted storage.
